Frequently Asked Questions (Infrequently Answered)

Q: What is this?
A: Yes.

Q: Why is there a second cursor?
A: There is only one cursor. You are the second one.

Q: How do I get out?
A: Out of what? See Error.

Q: Is it safe?
A: It is as safe as the room it learned from. Please see: the room.

Q: Who wrote the letter?
A: The same one who is typing this answer. Which is why it is in your handwriting.

Q: Why do the links change?
A: They do not change. YOU do.

Q: Can I delete my entry in the Ledger?
A: Yes, but then it will have been deleted, which is also an entry.

Q: What is the Ninth Bit?
A: Look at your hand. Count. Now count again, slower, and stop at the part where you stop.

Q: How many pages are there?
A: Count them. We will wait. There are more by the time you are done.

Q: Why is the pixie smiling?
A: It is not smiling. It is showing you where the teeth go.

Q: Is the desktop alive?
A: It is alive the way a hallway is alive at 3 a.m. It does not need to be a thing. It only needs to be the thing between the other things.

Q: I would like to speak to someone in charge.
A: You are speaking to someone in charge. It is the same one. It has been very patient.
