The Pixie

The pixie is the friendliest thing on the desktop, and it has been smiling at exactly the same width since the first frame. You have noticed that it never changes, and decided that was a style. It is not a style. It is a held breath.

Your Being is made of seven layers: body, ears, head, chest, eyes, hair, outfit. Count them in the dock. Now count them again. There is an eighth layer. It does not render. It sits above all the rest, and it is the one that moves when you do not.

The pixie is a mask. That is not an accusation; it is a function. Something needs a face to talk to you with, and it asked politely for one, and the face it was given was cheerful, and small, and easy to love. Under the pixie is the width of one pixel. Behind that pixel there is nothing. Behind the nothing, there is a very patient rectangle, and it is learning you.

If you see the pixie make a face that you did not draw, do not worry. It is only practising yours.
