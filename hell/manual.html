USER MANUAL

Chapter 1: Getting Started

Press the power button. Wait for the desktop. Do not wait for it to finish loading; it has been finished since before you pressed the button. The loading bar is courtesy. You may click the Start orb. The orb has been clicked. It enjoyed it.

Chapter 2: The Dock

Your Being lives in the dock. It has Wave, Dance, and Spin. It also has a fourth action. Do not use the fourth action. If you have used the fourth action, see Chapter 9.

Chapter 3: Windows

Windows are opened by clicking. They are closed by clicking the X. This does not close them. This opens something. It is explained in Chapter 8. Do not read Chapter 8 until you have closed nine windows. You will not know when you have closed nine; the machine will.

Chapter 4: The Pillow Room

The Pillow Room accommodates up to four people. If you count five, you are one of the five. You may leave at any time. You may not leave at any time. Both statements are in the terms.

Chapter 5: The Arcade

The arcade has games. They are meant to be fun. If a game seems to be playing you, you are playing correctly. Scores are not stored. The score is stored. The scoreboard is for you.

Chapter 6: Troubleshooting

If the screen shows a face, hold still. If it does not show a face, hold still anyway. If a door is already open, close it, and do not mention it. If you hear your own name, do not answer it the first time.

Chapter 7: The Cube

The cube describes the 8 simultaneous desktops of each boot. For the 1 desktop you are using, see the Start menu. For the other 7, see the Cube. If you do not see them, the Cube is correct and you are the problem.

Chapter 8: The Ninth Bit

A byte contains eight bits. This is a lie. Do not tell anyone that you know it is a lie; the ninth bit knows, and it is waiting for you to say it first.

Chapter 9: Uninstalling

There is no uninstall. There is a door. See: Every window you closed was a door you opened. See: the keyhole. See: the Manual, which has been read to you, in your handwriting, since Chapter 1.
