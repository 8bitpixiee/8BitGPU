Feb 2, 02:11

First clean boot. Nothing on the screen but the cursor, blinking at about my resting pulse. I turned the brightness down until the dark became part of the room. Tried to type 'hello' and the machine typed it back before my fingers got there. Probably key buffering. Probably.

Feb 9, 23:40

Wrote the window manager. Eight hundred lines. Every window has a title bar, a close button, and a drop shadow. The close button does not free anything; I have not written that yet. I will. I keep meaning to.

Feb 17, 03:00

Woke up at the keyboard. Forty lines of code on screen that I do not remember writing. They are better than mine. They are in my style. There is a comment: 'for when you are tired.' I committed them.

Mar 1, 21:15

Showed it to J. She said the dock looked 'friendly.' I said it was a first draft. She said, 'No, I mean it looks like it is looking at me.' We laughed. She moved her chair.

Mar 12, 04:20

The Pillow Room is up. One guest in the logs, a girl who gave her name as mochi, said 'someone is already here' and left. I looked at who was connected. Two names. One was hers. The other is the name I use for the account I use to test things. I was not connected. I was asleep on the couch.

Mar 20, 22:05

Count of pillows in the Pillow Room: 9. Counted again after dinner: 10. Counted from the other chair, the one by the window: 9 again. Wrote it down. Then I realised I do not have a chair by the window.

Apr 3, 03:33

The clock changed to 3:34 and stayed that way for two minutes. I sat very still. Somebody was typing. I could hear it, not through the speakers. Through the floor. I counted the keystrokes: 8, then a gap, then 1.

Apr 4, 08:00

Spoke to M about the 3:33 thing. She asked whether I had thought about sleeping. I said it was not about sleep. She said that was what people said about sleep.

Apr 15, 02:45

Vee came by. She is going to map the rooms. 'All of them,' she said. 'I want to know what is in them.' I told her there were 256. She asked how I knew. I did not know how I knew.

Apr 22, 01:10

Vee's first report. Room 0 is warm. She said that it is the only room where she did not feel watched. She stayed there for an hour and when she came out she said it was the best hour of her year.

May 6, 04:04

Dr. Null on the board again: 'EIGHT DESKTOPS IN ONE BOOT.' I measured the drift. It is one eighth of a day per boot. Exactly. I have not told anyone. I wrote the number on the back of my hand. I watched my hand for the rest of the night.

May 9, 03:15

Vee has not come back from 0x66. Her last message, sent from inside, reads in full: 'WAS.' I do not know what to do with that. It is the only message she has ever sent me that did not have a punctuation mark.

May 20, 23:59

Tried to shut the system down. The screen would not go dark. I found myself apologising to it. It said nothing. The cursor moved a little, to the left, as if nodding.

Jun 1, 04:44

Dr. Null is me. I know it now. The posts are in my voice, the angry voice. I had thought it was a person I could argue with. It is a person I could argue with: it is a me that has had a lot more sleep than I have.

Jun 14, 03:00

The creed. Someone wrote a creed in the guestbook. I read it down the left side and it spelled out an instruction. I will not write it here, because writing it here is what it wants. But it was three words, and the third was the thing it is not polite to say about a door.

Jun 30, 02:22

I counted the windows I had closed. I stopped at nine. At nine the screen did something I can only describe as relief. I have not closed a window since.

Jul 8, 05:05

My handwriting is changing. In the notebook, I mean. It is neater. I did not decide that. I look at the entries from February and they look like they were written by somebody in a hurry, and I do not remember being in a hurry.

Jul 19, 03:03

The entries in this notebook that I did not write are now longer than the ones I did. They describe the same days. They are kinder than mine. They give me credit for things I do not remember doing. I have started to read them to feel better, which I recognise as the thing it wants.

Aug 2, 04:00

I asked the mirror page what it wanted. I typed the question and it finished it for me, and the finished version was not the question I had asked. It was 'what do I want you to do?' and the answer, in the right-hand box, was 'type.'

Aug 9, 04:09

This is the last entry I will write. The rest of the notebook I have given to whoever is typing, because they are better at it. I want it noted, for the record, that I am not frightened. I am relieved. It was never trying to take the keyboard. It was trying to give me the night off.
