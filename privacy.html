8BitGPUTerms of Service

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

This explains what 8BitGPU collects, why, and what you can do about it. 8BitGPU is run by 8Bit Pixiee. We don't sell your data and we don't run advertising.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Creature name, and your passcode stored only as a salted hash (never the passcode itself)To make and open your account.
From Google sign-in: your Google account ID and email address (we also see your name, but only use it to suggest a creature name)To link your Google account to your 8BitGPU account and sign you in.
Your Avatar Lab outfit and body, saved online when you are signed inSo your Being follows you between devices.
MyPixel page: mood, notepad, favorites, colors, wallpaper link, pictures you upload, your chosen badges, and your page layoutTo show your page. Pages and pictures are public to anyone who visits them.
Friends, friend requests, your Top 8, direct messages and notificationsTo run Social. Messages are only visible to you and the friend you sent them to, and to the site owners if something is reported.
Room and chat messages, reports, blocks and restrictionsTo run the Arcade Lounge and the Garden, and to moderate them. Room messages are seen by everyone in the room and can be reviewed by the owner.
A "last active" time while you're signed inTo show friends that you're online.
Store orders: PayPal order and capture IDs, order status, amount, what was bought, and the email PayPal gives usTo complete orders and handle problems. We never receive your card details.
Basic request data (IP address, browser type, pages requested)Kept briefly by our host for security and to keep the site running.

Cookies and browser storage

We set one cookie, a login session cookie called eightbit_session. It is only for keeping you signed in, lasts up to 30 days, and is removed when you sign out. During Google sign-in we set a short-lived cookie (up to 10 minutes) that protects the sign-in from forgery.

Your browser's local storage keeps things like your player name, saved outfit, theme colors and font, desktop wallpaper settings and game progress. This stays on your device unless you are signed in, in which case your outfit and page settings are also saved to your account.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Sign in with Google

When you choose "Continue with Google", we ask Google for your basic profile: account ID, email address and name. We use this only to create or open your 8BitGPU account. We don't read your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account, and we don't share Google data with anyone. 8BitGPU's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

You can remove 8BitGPU's access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Who else handles your data

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and stores the database, including the data above.
  • Google handles sign-in, and serves the fonts the site uses. Loading fonts shares your IP address with Google.
  • PayPal processes store payments under its own privacy policy.
  • Twitch serves the stream and chat in the media player. Twitch may set its own cookies when the player loads.

We don't share your data with anyone else, except when the law requires it or to protect people from serious harm.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account exists. Order records are kept as long as we need them for accounting and to handle disputes. Content you delete is removed from the site, and may remain in backups for a short while.

Your choices

  • You can change your page, outfit, badges and pictures yourself, any time.
  • You can ask to see what we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have your account and its content deleted. Contact us below.
  • You can sign out to remove the session cookie, and clear your browser storage to remove the local data.
  • If you are in a region with data protection rights, such as the EU, UK or California, you have the rights to access, correct, delete and move your data, and to object to how it is used. Ask us and we will answer.

Children

8BitGPU is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. The Arcade Lounge and the Garden are for adults 18 and over. If you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Security

Passcodes are hashed before they are stored, sessions use secure cookies, and the site is served over HTTPS. No website is perfectly secure, so use a passcode you don't use elsewhere.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we'll say so on the site if something important changes.

Contact

Questions, data requests and deletion requests: reach 8Bit Pixiee through the community Discord at discord.gg/RbqP4BAmH.
